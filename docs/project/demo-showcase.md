# Project Demo Showcase

## Overview

The Project Demo Showcase is the public-facing presentation of your final prototype.

> [!WARNING]
> Each student must complete and submit the [TCPS Ethics Certification](project/ethics-certification.md) before participating in any course project work.

## Changelog

- Aug 7: added submission details.
- Aug 4: added details and rubric.

During the showcase, your team will demonstrate your prototype, explain your design process, and answer questions from visitors, peers, TAs, and the teaching team.

Visitors should be able to understand:

- what problem your team addressed
- who the design is for
- what your prototype does
- how the prototype supports your design goals
- what your team learned through evaluation

## Attendance and Participation

All group members are expected to attend and participate in person.

Half of each team will present during Round 1, while the other half presents in Round 2.

Absence tokens cannot be used for the final showcase. Refer to the syllabus for the workshop attendance and participation policy.

Each team member should be prepared to explain the project, demonstrate the prototype, and answer questions.

Additionally, each team member completes the Showcase Passport and Peer Feedback Qualtrics.

For help assigning demo roles, planning backups, and making sure every team member is prepared, see the [Group Work Resources](group-work-resources.md).

## Showcase Format

Each team will run a demo station. Each presentation will be 15-20 minutes long, where 5-7 minutes will be dedicated to the design process and context. 

Your demo may be:

- a guided scripted demo
- a semi-guided walkthrough
- a hands-on interactive demo
- a combination of presentation and visitor interaction

Choose the format that best helps visitors understand and experience your prototype.

## Demo Preparation

Come prepared with:

- a working prototype
- any device, adapter, charger, or accessory needed to run it
- a short demo script or plan
- a backup plan in case of technical issues
- a short visual summary of your project

## Rubric
<details class="rubric">
<summary><strong>Complete</strong></summary>

**1. Professional showcase attendance and engagement (0.5 points)**  
Full team is present for the entire showcase, set up and ready before Round 1 starts. The team maintains a professional, energetic presence throughout, and delivers a clear, concise pitch to audience.

**2. Presentation of design process and problem context (2 points)**  
Team clearly and consistently explains the design problem, who the design is for, what the prototype does, how it supports the design goals, and what the team learned through evaluation.

**3. Public demo readiness of the prototype (0.5 points)**  
Prototype runs reliably through repeated demonstrations without breaking or needing a reset. Visitors can interact with it smoothly with little to no guidance from the team.

**4. Participation in project feedback reviews (1 point)**  
Every team member visits and meaningfully reviews other teams' projects, and completes the Showcase Passport and Peer Feedback Qualtrics.

</details>
<details class="rubric">
<summary><strong>Partially Complete</strong></summary>

**1. Professional showcase attendance and engagement (0.5 points)**  
Most of the team attends most of the showcase (e.g., one member absent or leaves early), presence/energy dips at points, or the pitch is present but unclear or unrehearsed.

**2. Presentation of design process and problem context (2 points)**  
One or two points of the context (the design problem, who the design is for, what the prototype does, how it supports the design goals, and what the team learned through evaluation) are missing, unclear, or is only addressed at the surface level. 

**3. Public demo readiness of the prototype (0.5 points)**  
Prototype mostly works but has noticeable issues (occasional resets, a secondary flow breaking), or requires unintended team guidance (ex. unplanned Wizard-of-Oz) for visitors to interact with it successfully.

**4. Participation in project feedback reviews (1 point)**  
Most but not all members complete reviews/submissions, or submissions for the the Showcase Passport and Peer Feedback Qualtrics are made but minimal or low-effort.

</details>
<details class="rubric">
<summary><strong>Incomplete</strong></summary>

**1. Professional showcase attendance and engagement (0.5 points)**  
Team members are absent, arrive late to the point of disrupting the showcase, or has no coherent pitch ready for visitors.

**2. Presentation of design process and problem context (2 points)**  
Team struggles to explain the design process and problem context; several elements are missing, and a visitor without background knowledge would leave confused.

**3. Public demo readiness of the prototype (0.5 points)**  
Prototype breaks often, can't be used by visitors without unintended team guidance (ex. unplanned Wizard-of-Oz), or is non-functional for long periods of the showcase.

**4. Participation in project feedback reviews (1 point)**  
Team largely skips reviewing other projects, and/or required feedback is not submitted.

</details>
